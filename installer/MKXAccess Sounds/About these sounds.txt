MKX Access sounds

Every sound the mod plays is one .wav file in this folder. To use your own
sound, replace a file with yours and keep the same name. A 16-bit .wav plays
at the volume you set in the accessibility menu; other .wav kinds play at
their own volume. If a file is missing, that sound is just skipped.
Restart the game after replacing a file. Any 16-bit .wav works, mono or
stereo, at any sample rate.

you75.wav        your health dropped to 75 percent
you50.wav        your health dropped to 50 percent
you25.wav        your health dropped to 25 percent
opponent75.wav   your opponent dropped to 75 percent
opponent50.wav   your opponent dropped to 50 percent
opponent25.wav   your opponent dropped to 25 percent
                 (all six start out as the same health sound; it comes from the
                 side of that fighter's health bar)
xray_you.wav     your super meter is full: X-ray ready
xray_opponent.wav  your opponent's X-ray is ready (starts out as the same sound)
wall_loop.wav           steady hum while either fighter is close to a wall, louder the closer they
                        get, from that wall's side (it repeats, so a replacement should loop smoothly)
menu_open.wav    the accessibility menu opened
menu_close.wav   the accessibility menu closed
menu_move.wav    moved to another item in the menu
menu_edge.wav    went past the last or first item (the menu wraps around)
